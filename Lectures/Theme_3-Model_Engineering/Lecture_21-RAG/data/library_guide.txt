Maple Hollow Public Library - Visitor Guide

About the Library

The Maple Hollow Public Library is a small community library located at 214 Birch Street, in the center of the town of Maple Hollow. The library was founded in 1952, when a group of local teachers donated their personal book collections to the town. Today the library holds about 38,000 books, 2,500 DVDs, and 120 board games. The library is run by a team of six librarians and about twenty volunteers. The head librarian is Ms. Rosalind Okafor, who has worked at the library since 2009.

Opening Hours

The library is open from 9 AM to 7 PM on Monday through Thursday, and from 9 AM to 5 PM on Friday. On Saturday the library is open from 10 AM to 4 PM. The library is closed on Sundays and on public holidays. During the summer months of July and August, the library closes one hour earlier on weekdays.

Library Membership

Membership in the library is free for all residents of Maple Hollow. Visitors who live outside of the town can become members for an annual fee of 25 dollars. To get a library card, a new member needs to bring a photo ID and a document that shows their home address, such as a utility bill. Children under the age of 12 can get a library card with the signature of a parent or guardian. A lost library card can be replaced at the front desk for a fee of 3 dollars.

Borrowing Rules

Members can borrow up to 8 items at the same time. Books can be borrowed for three weeks, while DVDs and board games can be borrowed for one week. Members can renew a borrowed item up to two times, either online or at the front desk, as long as no other member has placed a hold on the item. New releases are marked with a green sticker, and they can be borrowed for only one week and cannot be renewed.

Late Fees

The library charges a late fee of 25 cents per day for each overdue book, and 1 dollar per day for each overdue DVD or board game. The maximum late fee for a single item is 10 dollars. Members who owe more than 15 dollars in late fees cannot borrow new items until the fees are paid. Children's books are exempt from late fees. Once a year, during the Food for Fines week in December, members can pay their late fees by donating canned food to the local food bank.

Study Rooms and Computers

The library has four study rooms on the second floor, and each room can seat up to six people. Study rooms can be reserved for up to two hours per day, and reservations can be made online or by phone up to one week in advance. The library also has 12 public computers and free Wi-Fi. Printing costs 10 cents per black-and-white page and 50 cents per color page.

Events and Programs

The library hosts a story time for young children every Wednesday at 10:30 AM. A book club for adults meets on the first Tuesday of every month at 6 PM, and new members are always welcome. On Saturday afternoons, volunteers offer free help with computers and smartphones for senior citizens. Every spring the library organizes a used book sale, and the money raised is used to buy new books for the children's section.

Contact Information

The library can be reached by phone at 555-0142, or by email at info@maplehollowlibrary.example. Questions about events and programs can be sent to Mr. Daniel Reyes, the events coordinator. The library also posts news and updates about upcoming events on its website.
